# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Patients and families in Chinchwad, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and greater Pune deciding where to trust a surgical procedure or their family's dental care. They arrive anxious and comparison-minded, often on a phone, often referred by a relative. They are evaluating one thing above all: can I trust these hands? They want to feel the practice is established, expert, and unhurried before they ever call.

## Product Purpose

A small multi-page marketing site for Chintamani Hospital & Dental Clinic that converts that first anxious visit into a booked consultation. It carries two care lines under one roof: Dr. Tushar Khachane (general and laparoscopic surgery, GI endoscopy) and Dr. Babita Khachane (dental). A home page introduces the practice; each doctor has a dedicated, specialty-customized page. The whole site is trilingual (English, Marathi, Hindi) because the audience is Pune-local. Success is a patient who lands, feels reassured within five seconds, understands the care offered, and calls or requests a callback.

## Brand Personality

Established, exact, and human. The voice of a senior surgeon who explains clearly and never oversells: plain sentences, specific claims, no hype. Three words: trustworthy, exacting, warm. The site should feel like a respected institution that still knows your name.

## Anti-references

- The generic teal/blue medical-SaaS template: a thin accent on white, identical rounded cards in a 3-up grid, stock smiling doctors. The brand colour IS blue (sampled from the logo), so the defence is not avoiding blue but committing it: deep brand-blue anchor bands, navy serif headlines, the real doctors' faces, real Google reviews, trilingual copy. If it could be any clinic's template, restart.
- Spa / wellness softness: pastels, thin delicate type, lifestyle calm. This is surgery and dentistry, not a retreat. Stay clinical-premium, not cosmetic.
- Cold hospital-chain corporate: sterile, stocky, faceless. We are a senior husband-and-wife practice, not a chain.
- Off-white "AI paper" canvas (the Claude / cream look). The client explicitly rejected it. The canvas is pure white.
- Heavy 3D / particle gimmickry. Motion is subtle and earns its place.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the product.** Every decision is judged by whether an anxious patient trusts these hands more. Credentials, real faces, plain language, and composure over flash.
2. **Commit the color.** Brand blue (sampled from the logo swoosh) is the brand, not a thin accent; navy (the wordmark) is the typographic ink. Blue carries the anchor bands; pure white carries the reading. Restraint that reads as "safe" is failure.
3. **Show the people.** Real photography of the actual doctors and rooms does more for trust than any graphic. No stock faces, no colored placeholder blocks.
4. **Say less, exactly.** Specific, concrete copy. No marketing buzzwords, no aphorisms, no filler. If deleting 30% improves it, delete.
5. **Not generic, on purpose.** If a competitor's site could carry the same screenshot, restart. The Bricolage Grotesque headline voice, the committed logo-blue, the real faces and reviews, and the composition are the signature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum: body text >= 4.5:1 on white, large text >= 3:1, visible focus, full keyboard paths, 44px touch targets. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (every animation degrades to instant/static). Readable for an older demographic: body >= 16px, generous line-height, no light-gray-on-white body. Fully trilingual (English / Marathi / Hindi) via Astro i18n routing + hreflang; Devanagari gets extra line-height. Marathi is first-class (Pune is Marathi-first).
